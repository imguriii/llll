Profit margin

Your estimate per dollar sold.

How it works

C2I buys add inventory with ₹0 profit. I2C sales use that inventory and your saved margin.

Example: selling $40 at ₹3 per dollar estimates ₹120 profit. Changing the margin affects future sales.

This is a margin estimate, not a calculation of actual purchase costs, fees, or net income.
